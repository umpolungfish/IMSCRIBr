Add render_wiring_ascii: terminal logical-circuit diagram, edge weights explicit

A terminal counterpart to render_wiring_svg_v3, sharing the same wire graph and
register simulation. Draws the port-level circuit node by node, one connector
per outgoing wire, and makes the edge weight explicit as the Belnap register
delta the SVG already carries: ↑ true, ↓ false, ↑↓ both, · none, with «B» for a
paradox held, ⚡ for a truth flip, and a heavy ═▶ for a cross-branch wire. IFIX
is shown ⊡ to match the ob3ect glyph.
