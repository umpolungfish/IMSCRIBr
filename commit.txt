The lift reads LaTeX and runs on this project's own inference

Two things kept the lift from being usable on a manuscript.

It classified sections through an outside service and required a key for it, so a
structural tool depended on an account, and it failed with a payment error rather than
a fingerprint. The native lane is the default now, through the ask binary, needing no
key and nothing external; naming a model still routes to the service that serves it.

And it read only Markdown headings. A LaTeX manuscript has none, so the parser fell
through to a paragraph split and imscribed the preamble instead of the paper. It now
reads the document body, takes the abstract as the opening section and each \section
as a section, and strips the markup so the classifier sees prose rather than macros.
The manuscript parses as eight sections, which is the arrangement length.
