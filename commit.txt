AREV and AFWD are now the marks ≺ and ≻

They were < and >, the only two of the twelve that arrived already meaning
something else: shell redirection, XML, LaTeX relations, Rust generics — and,
where it actually bit, 36 angle-bracket placeholders in the design prompt itself,
so the same characters were a mark and a slot in one text.

≺ and ≻ are unclaimed and read as precedes/succeeds, which is what a forward and
a reverse morphism are.

Only mark bindings changed. Struct endianness in Vox/vox.py and the folio-tag
parsing in manuscript_zfct.py use the same characters for their own reasons and
were left alone. < and > still READ as AFWD and AREV so words written before this
resolve; nothing writes them. The momonados-proof-* snapshots are crystallized
and untouched.
