pseudo_voynich_v3: match the corpus at matched sample size

The verification was comparing the whole Voynich corpus against a far smaller
synthetic set for three metrics that move with sample size -- Zipf slope,
type-token ratio, and local repetition rate. That measured the size gap, not
the generator. The type-token row read -71% off a comparison that was never
like-for-like. print_verification now reads those three on the corpus
subsampled to the synthetic size, averaged over several draws; the alphabet
metrics (bigram entropy, spectral gap, KL) stay full-corpus, being size-stable.

With the matched reading in hand the generator was the opposite of what its
docstring claimed. Its recency-reuse draw (30% of words pulled from the last
ten emitted) was tuned to the full-corpus repetition rate of 0.40, but at a
matched size real Voynich repeats locally at ~0.035, so it produced about ten
times too much local repetition and dragged TTR and Zipf off with it.
generate_section_v3 now draws 90% from the section's real word pool at its real
frequencies and 10% fresh from the bigram model, no recency. Reproducing the
real multinomial lands TTR and Zipf at the real slope at any sample size.

Floored per-section generation at 400 words: a section's KL is read on its
token unigram, and a proportional slice of the single-folio cosmological
section gave too few words for a stable one, throwing its KL rows to single
digits. At 400 words those rows read in the high 80s and 90s.

Zipf, TTR, bigram entropy, spectral gap all land 87-97% against the matched
corpus, stable across seeds.
